An Open Letter

The Journey of Rebirth –
Paving the Future

Did you know that every discarded tire takes hundreds of years to decompose naturally? Here in Tây Ninh, we decided to shorten that meaningless wait by giving them a new, brilliant, and more valuable lifecycle.

Welcome to GreenPath Tây Ninh.

We are a team of engineers and strategic experts driven by the aspiration to realize the circular economy model. Witnessing roads constantly rutting under the harsh sun, side by side with piles of old tires abandoned in landfills, we realized that the infrastructure construction industry desperately needs a materials revolution.

Through breakthrough polymer composite technology, we have created "rubber roads." We seamlessly blend standard bitumen with crumb rubber derived from old tires. This process completely transforms the material, significantly enhancing its mechanical properties to yield a high-performance modified bitumen resistant to extreme load and deformation.

"Every kilometer of road paved with GreenPath's materials means thousands of tires are rescued from industrial incinerators. Join us in laying down these green ribbons."

The GreenPath Team

Tây Ninh, Vietnam

Our Core Technology

Engineered for Extreme Durability

By manipulating polymer structures at the microscopic level, we turn an environmental liability into an infrastructure asset.

Composite Blending

Advanced thermal blending of standard bitumen and crumb rubber maximizes the mechanical resistance against extreme deformation, rutting, and heavy traffic loads.

Eco-Processing

State-of-the-art, zero-emission tire shredding and cryogenic treatment facilities ensure our modified bitumen leaves virtually no carbon footprint.

Infrastructure Longevity

Dramatically increasing the highway lifecycle reduces ongoing road maintenance costs, pothole patching frequency, and material consumption.

Commercial Solutions

Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen

GreenPath Tây Ninh manufactures high-performance Modified Bitumen. By seamlessly blending asphalt with recycled tire rubber, we enhance its mechanical properties to create a composite mixture with superior load-bearing capacity and unmatched resistance to road deformation. Available in tailored packaging for any application.

Plastic Bucket 5/10/20kg

Packaging: Sealed Pail

Convenient, heavy-duty plastic buckets designed for airtight storage and easy manual handling. Perfect application: Ideal for highly mobile maintenance crews, fast pothole patching, crack sealing, and localized minor road repairs without the need for large equipment.

Plastic Drum 100kg

Packaging: Sealed Drum

Airtight plastic drums designed to safely preserve the rubber-bitumen mixture and block moisture. Perfect application: Specialized hot-mix asphalt plants and projects requiring precise mixing ratios for long-term road surfacing.

Jumbo Bag 500kg

Packaging: FIBC Bulk Bag

Heavy-duty bulk bags engineered for high-volume efficiency and rapid deployment. Perfect application: Heavy-duty expressway paving, national highways, and continuous large-scale infrastructure construction demanding massive load-bearing strength.
